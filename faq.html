Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders reach Karachi in 2 to 3 days and the rest of Pakistan in 3 to 5 working days.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available in all major cities.

How do I exchange an item?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unworn with tags attached.

Which sizes do you offer?

We stock S to XL. Each product page lists the size options available.

Do you ship internationally?

Not yet. We currently deliver within Pakistan only.
